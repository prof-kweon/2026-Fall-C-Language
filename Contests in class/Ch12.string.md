# Long Text: Length and the Word "my"

**Time limit:** 1 s &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Memory limit:** 64 MB &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Points:** 100

---

Your program has a long text inside it. It does not read any input.
Find the length of the text and count the word **my** in it.

## The text
The text is already in your code. Do not change it.
```
My day begins early, and my dog waits by the door. I pack my bag, grab my coat, and walk with my friend Mia to the bus stop. She laughs at my jokes, but MY teacher does not! Oh my! Yesterday my class read a mystery about an army of ants, and I told everyone about my family. Is my story true? Ask my grandmother, my uncle, or my neighbor. I made this lunch myself, and my sandwich is the best one in school. My secret? Smile, keep my promise, and share my snacks. Oh my, tomorrow my brother comes too!
```
Keep it in an array:
```c
char text[] = "<the text above>";
```
Do not write `char *text = "..."`. `strtok` changes the text, and that kind of string cannot be changed.

The text has only English letters, spaces and these marks: `,` `.` `!` `?` `;`

## What to do
1. Find the length of the text. Spaces and marks count.
2. Count the word `my`.
3. Print the two numbers.

## The word "my"
- A word is a group of English letters. Spaces and the marks `,` `.` `!` `?` `;` are between words.
- `My`, `my`, `MY` and `mY` are the same word.
- Only the exact word counts. `myself`, `mystery` and `army` are not `my`.
- `Oh my!` has the word `my`.

## Rules
The judge reads your code.
- Use `#include <string.h>`.
- Use `strlen`, `strtok` and `strcmp` (or `strncmp`, `strcasecmp`). Do not write your own.

## Hints
- `strtok(text, " ,.!?;")` gives the first word. `strtok(NULL, " ,.!?;")` gives the next word, and `NULL` at the end.
- `tolower()` (from `<ctype.h>`) makes a letter lower case.

## Output
There is no input. Print only these two lines:
```
Length: <length of the text>
Count: <how many times my is in the text>
```

## Example (a different, short text)
Text:
```
Oh my, my dog and MY cat; mystery is not my game.
```
Output:
```
Length: 49
Count: 4
```
`mystery` is not `my`. Only 4 words count.
